Welcome to Week 2. During this week we have the first seminar for the module and will focus on discussing the fundamental elements of first order or predicate logic. It will involve students engaging with this underpinning mathematical notation in order to provide a greater understanding of some of the key elements of intelligent agents and wider topics. 

## Learning outcomes

- Understand the core elements of first order logic.
- Develop an awareness of creating and reasoning over first order logic.
