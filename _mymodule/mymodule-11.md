Welcome to Week 11. This unit will bring together several of the core subjects that have been covered to date, such as agent-based computing, adaptive algorithms and deep learning. This week focuses on how these technologies are currently being used in practice in manufacturing and Industry 4.0, specifically manufacturing and FinTech, to great effect. 

## Learning outcomes
- Apply the concepts and theories learned to date to specific sectors.
- Understand how technology can be used to drive efficiency.
- Evaluate the relative merits of these new approaches in context.
