Welcome to Week 7. This week’s lecturecast focuses on one of the most rapidly growing technologies in intelligent agents, which is Natural Language Processing (or NLP). The lecturecast covers some of the background to NLP and why it is hard for machines to understand the way that we talk before diving into some examples of methods and technologies that can be deployed.

## Learning outcomes
- Evaluate the difficulties involved in developing and deploying NLP systems.
- Understand the core principles of NLP technologies.
