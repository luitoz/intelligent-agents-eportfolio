Welcome to Week 9. This week’s lecturecast introduces adaptive algorithms, such as Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs) and Deep Learning. ## Learning outcomes
- Understand the core concepts of artificial neural networks.
- Appraise the relative strengths of these techniques.
