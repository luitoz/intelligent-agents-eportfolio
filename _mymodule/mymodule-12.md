Welcome to Week 12. This week’s seminar is focused on exploring the future of deep learning technologies. We will build on everything that we have learned to date to explore the possible future directions of intelligent technologies, particularly deep learning. Will advances in deep learning begin to shift towards things that were once considered the realms of science fiction? Students will discuss the technologies that they wrote about in Week 10 with the rest of the class, alongside some examples from the tutor.

## Learning outcomes
- Appraise current technologies.
- Assess the potential evolution of technology based on today’s examples.
- Evaluate the potential ethical and social considerations.
